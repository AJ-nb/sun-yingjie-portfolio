## Context

Connecting a Chinese identity to product concepts through boundaries, curves and touch.

## My role

Team collaboration · Identity, product concepts & CMF.

## Design problem

How can intimacy, ritual and personal boundaries inform both a recognisable identity and a coherent product direction?

## Design decisions

### Make the name a brand asset

The work moved from early Songnasty curves to Chinese lettering and seal-like order, giving the identity a stable centre.

![Historical Songnasty identity exploration](/works/brand/yelisi/songnasty-historical-logo.webp)

### Hold fluidity within a boundary

A near-square contour and seal-inspired strokes establish order; bodily curves emerge as a secondary reading in negative space.

![YELISI | user-supplied retrospective board 01 / 20](/works/boards/yelisi/yelisi-01.webp)

### Extend identity into objects

A snake-like body and butterfly components explore connection, combination and touch, translating brand semantics into product and CMF questions.

![YELISI | user-supplied retrospective board 01 / 20](/works/boards/yelisi/yelisi-01.webp)

## Outcome & stage

An identity direction centred on the Chinese name and seal-like order, extended into product and CMF studies. The products remain concepts.

## Reflection

Materials and contact mechanisms need physical validation. Magnetic retention, release, durability and ergonomics cannot be established by renders.
