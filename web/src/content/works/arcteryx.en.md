## Context

Translating terrain, bodies and equipment into spatial layers for the Alpha Center window display.

## My role

BENWU team · 3D design & spatial visualisation.

## Design problem

How can an outdoor performance context become a retail environment in which the relationship between body, equipment and terrain remains clear?

## Design decisions

### Start with terrain

Changes in height, angular lines and volumes establish a viewing sequence and give merchandise a clear place.

![Arc’teryx Mountain Performance Field | user-supplied retrospective board 01 / 10](/works/boards/arcteryx/arcteryx-01.webp)

### Organise direction and force

Diagonals, paths and visual centres connect spatial motion cues to the relationship between bodies and equipment.

![Return to display scale](/media/v7/brand/arcteryx-logic.webp)

### Return to display scale

Overall and detail views compare support, suspension, spacing and viewing angles. Installation views add spatial context.

![Arc’teryx Alpha Center installation, front window](/works/legacy/arcteryx/installation/arcteryx-installation-window-portrait.jpg)

## Reflection

A technical narrative needs readable relationships: support, direction and viewing position. Visual dynamism alone does not establish performance or engineering validation.
