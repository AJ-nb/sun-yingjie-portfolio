## Context

Connecting positioning, typographic identity, information hierarchy and product language for a photography-equipment context.

## My role

Team collaboration · Identity, applications & information systems.

## Design problem

How can a technically expressive identity keep product and application information clear?

## Design decisions

### Define positioning and reading order

Separate the roles of brand identity, product information and supporting communication around the needs of photography equipment.

![Periastra | user-supplied retrospective board 01 / 15](/works/boards/periastra/periastra-01.webp)

### Create stability through lettering

Compare letter structure, strokes, negative space and spacing to balance technical character with legibility.

![Periastra wordmark study](/works/brand/periastra/final-wordmark.png)

### Turn graphics into rules

Test hierarchy and product language at different application scales so that the identity remains coherent.

![Retrospective analysis of identity and application relationships](/media/v7/brand/periastra-logic.webp)
