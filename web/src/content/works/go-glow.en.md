## Background

GO GLOW begins with carrying, storing and charging personal-care products while traveling. The original proposal focuses on women and brings toothbrush, facial-cleansing and scalp-care directions into one product language, exploring the transition between everyday and travel use.

## My role

Contributed to a collaborative product concept covering use scenarios, module combinations, form, use and a companion interface. Original boards and collaboration information remain with the work. This edition uses the original product form to clarify the relationship between the physical system, interface and brand presentation.

## Key question

A common handle can unify carrying and storage, but brushing, facial cleansing and scalp care involve different contact areas. Attachments need clear identities and replacement, cleaning and storage sequences. Similar shapes do not imply identical hygiene or drive requirements.

![Packing, use and maintenance journey](/works/documents/portfolio-51/032.webp)

## Design process

### Derive the product relationships from carrying and use

The original journey moves from packing to use, cleaning and maintenance. A shared body reduces visual fragmentation, while attachments differ according to contact areas. Sketches compare the handle and module proportions; family views help assess whether the system is understandable.

### Keep consistency in the body and differences at the touchpoints

A common contour and color language connect the family. Toothbrush, facial-cleansing and scalp-care attachments differ at their ends. The decision is to give shared and dedicated parts clear roles, rather than making all modules look the same.

### Map the interface to the physical modules

Low- and high-fidelity screens add modes and information structure. Images, module names and states need to refer to the same functions. Connections, water resistance and cleaning performance require prototype validation.

![Modular personal-care sketches](/works/documents/portfolio-51/033.webp)

![Companion-app low- and high-fidelity screens](/works/documents/portfolio-51/035.webp)

## Final work

The final presentation combines care attachments with a consistent silhouette and color language, product arrangements and interface screens. Different end forms distinguish toothbrush, facial-cleansing and scalp-care uses; the shared body establishes family resemblance.

## Outcome and stage

The concept connects use scenarios, family form, module relationships and a companion interface. Brand applications in this edition use the original product imagery, retaining its form, proportions and structure while adapting format, space and information hierarchy. Connections, water resistance, cleaning and storage require prototypes and testing. An earlier background page follows.
