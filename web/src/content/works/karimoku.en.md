## Context

A study of how woodcraft, material and space can connect furniture, photography and communication.

## My role

Brand & material research · Spatial narrative.

## Design problem

How can a furniture brand’s craft and cultural context translate into relationships between materials, space and movement?

## Design decisions

### Translate culture into relationships

Wood, craft and everyday life inform the study of edges, joints and touch, beyond decorative shorthand.

![Karimoku × BENWU | user-supplied retrospective board 01 / 20](/works/boards/karimoku/karimoku-01.webp)

### Organise the void and the layers

Distances between furniture, walls and display elements create spatial order. Overall and detail views explain scale together.

![Karimoku × BENWU | user-supplied retrospective board 07 / 20](/works/boards/karimoku/karimoku-07.webp)

### Connect light and communication

Shadows, surface variation and photographic scale help preserve material and spatial relationships in communication.

![Karimoku × BENWU | user-supplied retrospective board 15 / 20](/works/boards/karimoku/karimoku-15.webp)

## Outcome & stage

A connected research framework spanning brand foundations, cultural translation, materials, void, movement, light and communication. Presented as a design study.

## Reflection

Restraint needs concrete decisions about distance, layers, edges and light. Material samples and original project records remain necessary for further validation.
