## Background

Water Guardian uses surface-algae collection and water maintenance as the context for combining a moving floating body, filtering openings and cleaning modules. Rounded volumes establish the overall form of the proposed equipment.

## My role

This is a collaborative project. The source credits include Deng Zhicheng (邓志成) and Zhang Chengcheng (张成承). The material covers sketches, floating-body layout, filtering and cleaning parts, orthographic views and scene renders.

## Key question

Surface intake, floating body and upper equipment need a clear vertical relationship and identifiable maintenance locations. A visually stable shape does not establish flotation stability, and an intake opening does not demonstrate algae-removal capacity.

## Design process

Sketches develop a rounded floating silhouette. Exploded and orthographic views locate the intake, internal modules and upper parts; water scenes show the proposed operating position.

This edition reads the layout through entry, treatment and maintenance, retaining the collaborative credits for Deng Zhicheng and Zhang Chengcheng. Actual buoyancy, filtration flow and ecological effects require operational and water-body evidence.

## Final work

The final board brings together the floating equipment, component relationships and a water-surface scenario, preserving the original proposal’s diagrams and visualization.

## Outcome and stage

The outcome is an algae-cleaning equipment concept board. Filtering, algae-removal methods, ecological effects and water-quality improvement have not been tested.
